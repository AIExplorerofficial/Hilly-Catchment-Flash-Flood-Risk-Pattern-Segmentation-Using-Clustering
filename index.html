THE PROBLEM

The same rainfall means different things in different valleys.

Monsoon cloudbursts, flash floods and landslides repeatedly hit the Himalayan states (e.g. Kedarnath 2013, Himachal Pradesh 2023), and the water continues downstream to the plains. A single regional rain alert is a blunt tool: terrain changes how much rain is ordinary, how much falls in extreme bursts, and how fast it runs off.

Research question: can 11 years of gridded rainfall and terrain be grouped into a few zones with distinct rainfall behaviour, so alert thresholds and rain-gauge effort can be set per zone instead of once for the whole region?

TRY IT · WHY ONE THRESHOLD FAILS

Apply one daily-rainfall threshold mm to every cell. Each cell’s own wet-day P99 is the rain exceeded on 1% of wet days.

threshold below the cell’s P99 → fires more often than 1-in-100 wet days (alert fatigue) threshold above it → fires less often (missed events)

P99 varies 5.7-fold (31.7 → 179.5 mm), so at any setting some terrain bands over-alert while others almost never alert. The rest of the page builds the zones step by step and tests whether they fix this.

STEP 5 — STRESS-TEST THE ZONES

Would the zones survive a different analyst?

Stability results compare repeated seeds, 80% subsamples, and distance choices. Leave-one-feature-out ARI measures how membership changes when one feature is removed.

QUESTIONAre the zones stable across seeds, subsamples and feature choices?
WHAT WE DIDRe-ran with different seeds, 80% subsamples, and leaving each feature out in turn; compared to reference labels.
CALCULATIONAdjusted Rand Index ARI = (RI − E[RI]) / (max RI − E[RI]); reference line 0.70.
FINDINGFull k = 4: seed ARI 0.90, subsample 0.96. Hills k = 3: 0.98 / 0.97. Most influential features: annual-rain CV (full k = 4, ARI 0.64) and relief (hills k = 3, 0.59). K-Means vs K-Medians agree less at full k = 4 (ARI 0.67) than hills k = 3 (0.97).

THE SOLUTION

One threshold per zone, not one for the mountain.

Using the full-region, K-Means, k = 4 zones, each zone’s median wet-day P99 becomes its screening threshold.

Grid cells coloured by zone (28.5–33.5°N, 75.5–81°E). Rust = priority foothill zone.

DID IT HELP? · CALCULATION

Mean absolute gap between a cell’s own P99 and the threshold applied to it:
one regional threshold (median P99 = mm): mm  →  zone thresholds: mm

reduction in mismatch. This measures how well one number per zone represents its cells’ rainfall extremes; it is not a measure of flood-prediction accuracy.

Scope and limits. No flood-event inventory was used, so zones are rainfall–terrain screening units, not validated flood-risk classes, and thresholds are climatological starting points, not operational warnings (official agency thresholds govern). Cluster names interpret profiles. Rainfall covers 2015–2025 only; 0.25° cells smooth out valley-scale variation. Original IMD/SRTM rasters and raw label CSVs were not included, so this site shows saved notebook outputs and does not re-run clustering.
